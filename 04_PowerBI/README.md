# 04 – Power BI Dashboards


Download:- https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ZswUz_XeANzYPLJdc6HlY7JwCd6egsJU?usp=sharing

This folder contains the interactive Power BI dashboards developed for the MediTrack Pharmacy Sales & Inventory Analytics project.
The dashboards transform pharmacy sales and inventory data into visual insights to support business performance monitoring and data-driven decision-making.

## Dashboard 1: Sales Performance Dashboard

### Key Performance Indicators
- Total Sales Revenue
- Total Invoices
- Total Quantity Sold
- Unique Medicines
- Average Invoice Value

### Key Visualizations
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue by Product Category
- Top 10 Medicines by Revenue
- Top 10 Manufactures by Revenue
- Revenue by Payment Type
- Discount Analysis
- Revenue by Shift
- Invoice Count by Payment Type 
- Invoices by Year Month

## Dashboard 2: Inventory Intelligence Dashboard

### Key Performance Indicators
- Total Stock In
- Total Stock Out
- Total Remaining Stock
- Negative Stock Records
- Zero Stock Records

### Key Visualizations
- Monthly Stock Movement Trend
- Stock Movement by Warehouse
- Stock Status Distribution
- Top 10 Medicines by Stock In
- Low & Negative Stock Details

## Tools and Techniques

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX Measures
- Calculated Columns
- Interactive Slicers
- Conditional Formatting
- Data Visualization

## Business Value

The dashboards help monitor pharmacy sales performance, identify inventory issues, analyse stock movements, and understand business trends through interactive visual reporting.
